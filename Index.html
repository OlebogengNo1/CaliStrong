The Science
// Why this works
📈
Progressive Overload
The #1 driver of muscle growth. Each week, make the exercise slightly harder — more reps, harder variation, slower tempo. Muscles grow only when forced to adapt to a new stimulus.
⚡
Mechanical Tension & Metabolic Stress
Calisthenics creates high mechanical tension (especially at end-range) and metabolic stress when taken close to failure. Both signals trigger muscle protein synthesis and hypertrophy.
📊
Volume Landmarks (MEV → MAV)
Dr. Mike Israetel's research defines Minimum Effective Volume (~10 sets/week) and Maximum Adaptive Volume (~20 sets/week). This program starts near MEV and ramps to MAV over months.
🎯
RIR — Reps in Reserve
Training 1–3 reps from failure maximises activation without CNS burnout. Beginners leave 3 RIR; advanced athletes push to 1 RIR on final sets. Never grind every set to absolute zero.
😴
Deload Every 4–6 Weeks
Reduce volume by ~40% every 4–6 weeks. Supercompensation happens during recovery — you grow between workouts, not during them. Skipping deloads leads to plateaus and overuse injury.
🥩
Nutrition Basics
Target 1.6–2.2g protein per kg of bodyweight daily. At ~83kg that's 133–183g. A slight caloric surplus (200–300 kcal) accelerates gains. Sleep 7–9hrs — growth hormone peaks during deep sleep.
